# SUL storefront redesign

Scope: homepage, collections, five existing product routes, shared navigation and bag. Visitor mode: Persuade. Use supplied photographs and preserve current product names, prices, sizes and links. The owner selected building the working website directly. Brand direction is an initial interpretation, open to owner steering and incoming assets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Walking past the SUL shop after hours becomes a web experience: garment studies and royal blue architectural fields lead directly to the garments.

OWN-WORLD: Royal blue, cool silver, graphite, photographic concrete. Condensed block lettering with quiet Manrope details. Square controls, generous portrait imagery, clean merchandising rows.

STORY: Discover the name and garments, explore the collection, inspect a piece, select a size, keep it in the bag. Preview commerce stays explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An unboxed three-part masthead above a graphite text field paired with tall chrome-and-blue garment imagery; Don't Sleep On Style owns the left, a large shop action below it, and a linked crown hoodie inset joins campaign to the studio. Mobile puts compact navigation, copy, action, and crop in reading order. Signature interaction: Studio / Styled swaps the whole product display between product photography and store context. Motion has a single cinematic image reveal and tactile controls.

FORM: Storefront threshold, candidate 6 of seven grounded forms: campaign magazine; sewn garment label; boutique gallery; tour merchandise; sportswear locker; storefront threshold; nightlife flyer. Seed 357c33ff. Challengers declined on identification and clarity: manga, fan page, algorave, teletext, Coptic binding, curved crease. Their retained disciplines are ordered visual storytelling, brand devotion, synchronized state change, navigational clarity, structural honesty, and decisive negative space, respectively. No borrowed costumes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
